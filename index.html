Frequently asked questions

See all questions & answers, with search.

How do I add today's photo?

Open the Journal tab and tap Take Photo or Library. You can add several photos per day with Add another photo.

How do I mark spots?

After adding a photo, the marking screen opens. Use Box to drag a box (or tap to drop one), Pen to color over an area, and Erase to remove a mark. Pinch to zoom. Tap a photo on the day page to edit its marks later.

How do I change a check-in?

On the day page, tap Edit next to Check-in.

I don't get the daily reminder.

Make sure it's on in the app under Settings → Daily reminder, and that notifications are allowed for SpeckLog in the iPhone Settings → Notifications. Focus modes can also silence it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

I bought SpeckLog Plus. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account. Plus usually comes back by itself; if not, open Settings → SpeckLog Plus (or the tip jar at the top right of Settings) and tap Restore Purchases. It's free: you never pay twice.

Important
