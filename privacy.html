Privacy Policy

Version 1.1

SpeckLog 1.1 for iPhone · Effective November 26, 2026

In short: Your journal is stored on your iPhone, with optional sync to your own private iCloud. The developer cannot access that journal. No SpeckLog account is required, and there are no analytics, ads or tracking. Information you choose to send by email is described below.

What the app stores, and where

To work as a skin journal, the app saves the following on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Photos you take or choose for each day
  • The spots you mark on those photos
  • Your daily check-in answers (stress, food and drink, sleep)
  • Skincare products and routines, treatments and their attachments, experiments, goals and hygiene records
  • Doctor and appointment details, documents and private insurance information you enter
  • Your reminder and appearance settings, and other app preferences
  • A background photo, if you choose one for the app's pages
  • A sound file you choose for a routine's background sound (copied into the app; it is not synced or backed up)

The app does not send this journal to the developer. Optional iCloud sync, paired-device features and exports are described below. The developer runs no journal server.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on Settings → Data → iCloud Sync, journal records and attachments are also stored in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account, for your other iPhones signed in to that account. The developer has no access to that private database. App preferences and recent photo-import records (time, photo count and album or file name) use your iCloud key-value storage. Preferences include an update-email name and address if you entered them. Background photos and custom routine sound files are not synced.

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on. Turning it off stops syncing; it does not itself delete the iCloud copy. You can manage that copy in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpeckLog.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only when you tap Take Photo. When you choose Library, Apple's photo picker lets you pick specific photos, and that picker gives the app only the photos you select. The separate album-import permission is described below. Photos are re-saved inside the app without their original embedded details, such as location.

Import Past Photos (Settings → Data) works the same way: you choose photos with Apple's picker, or image and .zip files in the Files app, and the app only receives those. To import a whole album, iOS asks you for access to your photo library; SpeckLog then lists your albums and shows previews. You choose which albums or photos to import. The app reads each photo's date taken to put it on the right day, then re-saves it without its original embedded details. Importing does not send your library to the developer. Imported journal photos can sync through iCloud if you enabled it. You can import without album access by choosing photos instead.

The app adds to your photo library only when you choose to: if you tap Save Video or Save Image when sharing a time-lapse video or an Insights image, or when you use Export All Photos. It never reads your library to do this. The one exception is optional: exporting your photos into a SpeckLog album. iOS only lets an app make an album with access to the library, so iOS asks you first; SpeckLog uses that access only to create the album and add your exported photos, and never reads, analyses or uploads your other photos. You can export without the album and grant add-only access instead.

Doctor appointments

When you tap Add to Calendar, SpeckLog opens Apple’s event editor with a generic appointment title and the date and time you entered. You choose whether and where to save it. SpeckLog does not read your calendar.

Sharing and exports

Photos, time-lapse videos, comparison/progress images, Insights exports and doctor reports are made on your iPhone. You choose where to save or send them. A report or backup may contain sensitive journal, treatment, doctor or insurance information; review the content and recipient before sharing. Exports are not automatically sent to the developer.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Widgets read a shared on-device snapshot of logged dates and spot counts. If you use the paired Apple Watch app, WatchConnectivity transfers routine names, steps and product names, and logged dates used for the streak between your iPhone and Watch. Routine completions can return to the iPhone. These features do not send your journal to the developer.

Research and external links

Research links and summaries are included in the app. Opening an external research, support or beta-survey link contacts that website, whose privacy practices apply. SpeckLog does not attach your journal to those links.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled as a local notification on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Analytics, ads and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs. It uses Apple's system services for features such as iCloud, purchases and paired-device communication. Sharing a file or opening an external link uses the destination you choose.

Purchases and tips

Apple processes SpeckLog Plus and tip purchases through StoreKit. The app reads verified transaction and entitlement information to unlock Plus, restore purchases and total tips. It stores unlock/tip bookkeeping on the device; purchase history and restoration use Apple's services. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or journal as part of a purchase. App Store rating prompts are also provided by Apple.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in those backups, just like other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple and protected by your Apple Account, not by the developer.

You can also export your own backup file in the app (Settings → Data → Export Backup). It contains journal records, photos, marks, attachments and supported settings, and goes only where you choose to save or send it; it is never uploaded to the developer. You can protect it with a password, which encrypts it on your device. Anyone with an unprotected file, or its password, can see its photos. Importing a backup reads it only on your device.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in the app under Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone. With iCloud Sync on, Delete All Data also removes it from iCloud and your other devices.

Feedback emails

“Send Feedback” (in Settings → Help, or when you long-press the app icon) opens an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. The draft includes the app version, iOS version and iPhone model to help with bugs; you can see and delete that before sending. Your email is used only to reply to you and is never shared.

Update emails

“Get update emails” (Settings → About) is optional. The name and email you type are saved as app preferences and may sync to your own iCloud when sync is on. Subscribe and Unsubscribe open a prepared email to the developer in your own mail app, and it is sent only if you send it yourself. At most one update email is sent per version, and it is never shared.

Children

The app does not automatically collect journal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.

Version 1.0

In short: SpeckLog does not collect any data. Your photos, marks and check-ins stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

To work as a skin journal, the app saves the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else (unless you turn on iCloud Sync, below, which keeps a copy in your own iCloud). The developer runs no servers and cannot see your data.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on Settings → Data → iCloud Sync, the same data (except the background photo and your settings) is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so it appears on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, protected like the rest of your iCloud data (and end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection). Only your Apple Account can read it: the developer has no access to it and runs no servers. iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on. Turning it off stops syncing; to remove the iCloud copy, go to the iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpeckLog.

Apple handles the payment and your payment details; the app and the developer never see them and collect nothing about your purchases.
