Privacy Policy

SpeckLog for iPhone · Effective November 8, 2026

In short: SpeckLog does not collect any data. Your photos, marks and check-ins stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

To work as a skin journal, the app saves the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Photos you take or choose for each day
  • The spots you mark on those photos
  • Your daily check-in answers (stress, food and drink, sleep)
  • Your reminder settings
  • A background photo, if you choose one for the app's pages

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else (unless you turn on iCloud Sync, below, which keeps a copy in your own iCloud). The developer runs no servers and cannot see your data.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on Settings → Data → iCloud Sync, the same data (except the background photo and your settings) is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so it appears on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, protected like the rest of your iCloud data (and end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection). Only your Apple Account can read it: the developer has no access to it and runs no servers. iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on. Turning it off stops syncing; to remove the iCloud copy, go to the iPhone's Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpeckLog.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only when you tap Take Photo. When you choose Library, Apple's photo picker lets you pick specific photos, and the app only receives the photos you select. It never sees the rest of your library. Photos are re-saved inside the app without their original embedded details, such as location.

The app adds to your photo library only when you choose to: if you tap Save Video or Save Image when sharing a time-lapse video or an Insights image, or when you use Export All Photos. It never reads your library to do this. The one exception is optional: exporting your photos into a SpeckLog album. iOS only lets an app make an album with access to the library, so iOS asks you first; SpeckLog uses that access only to create the album and add your exported photos, and never reads, analyses or uploads your other photos. You can export without the album and grant add-only access instead.

Sharing and exports

Time-lapse videos, Insights images and spreadsheets are made on your iPhone. They go only where you choose to save or send them from the share sheet, and are never sent to the developer.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled as a local notification on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Analytics, ads and third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and it does not share data with anyone.

Purchases and tips

SpeckLog Plus and tips are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; the app and the developer never see them and collect nothing about your purchases. The app only keeps a note on your iPhone of what you've unlocked. If the app asks for an App Store rating, that is also Apple's own prompt.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in those backups, just like other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple and protected by your Apple Account, not by the developer.

You can also export your own backup file in the app (Settings → Data → Export Backup). It contains your photos, marks and check-ins, and goes only where you choose to save or send it; it is never uploaded to the developer. You can protect it with a password, which encrypts it on your device. Anyone with an unprotected file, or its password, can see its photos. Importing a backup reads it only on your device.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in the app under Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone. With iCloud Sync on, Delete All Data also removes it from iCloud and your other devices.

Feedback emails

“Send Feedback” (in Settings → Help, or when you long-press the app icon) opens an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. The draft includes the app version, iOS version and iPhone model to help with bugs; you can see and delete that before sending. Your email is used only to reply to you and is never shared.

Update emails

“Get update emails” (Settings → About) is optional. The name and email you type stay on your iPhone. SpeckLog uploads nothing: Subscribe and Unsubscribe only open a prepared email to the developer in your own mail app, and it is sent only if you send it yourself. At most one update email is sent per version, and it is never shared.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud sync), this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
