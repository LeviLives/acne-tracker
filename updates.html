Version 1.1 In progress

Coming after 1.0
  • Move a photo to another day: took tonight’s photo just after midnight? Tap the photo’s ••• → Move to Another Day, pick the day, and choose what goes there: the photo and that day’s check-in, the photo only, or the check-in only. Changed your mind? Undo and Redo buttons appear at the bottom left.
  • Photos taken just after midnight now ask which day they’re for (the day before or today), then open the check-in for that day.
  • iCloud Sync is here (free): turn it on in Settings → Data to keep your diary in your own private iCloud, on all your iPhones.
  • Compare Days: Line up is smooth now, even with 8 photos, and asks whether to save before you leave.
  • Calendar: a “Back to this month” button when you’ve moved to another month.
  • Insights: Daily status (stress and sleep) now comes first, and the Compare / Totals switch is bigger.
  • Faster: the app does much less work after each change and while iCloud syncs. The Skincare Vault, Skincare today, adding a product and the perfect-week confetti are smooth now, even with years of data.
  • The check-in always shows how many spots you marked on that day’s photos, even with descriptions hidden.
  • Skincare routines with a timer: morning and evening routines with steps (wash, wait 10 minutes, treatment, moisturizer, take your medicine…). Start one and it runs itself, with a chime at each step, even with the screen off. Confirm at the end and it’s logged.
  • Skincare Vault: keep your products (type, brand, ingredients, photos) in Settings → Skincare, tap what you used in the daily check-in, and let “Use my routine” fill it in for you.
  • Patterns, at a glance: each hint is one short line with arrows and colors, only the strongest are shown, and good habits (veggies, water, sleep) are suggested to test too.
  • Skincare, smoother: tap a product’s photo to see it full screen (swipe to the ingredients list, pinch to zoom), pin favorites to the top, and replaced product photos wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Names and brands capitalize themselves, the product camera uses the back camera, and nothing you type is lost if you scroll or drag the sheet.
  • The check-in shows Skincare today right after sleep, with bigger Morning / Evening buttons; lots of products fit in a tidy 3 × 3 box.
  • The keyboard hides when you tap outside a text field or drag down, everywhere, and the tab bar no longer jumps up with it.
  • Routines, upgraded: Morning, Evening or General routines for Home or Travel; a short “Next: …” countdown between steps; several products per step (all logged when you finish); a Previous button; a chime when you start; and a “Start morning / evening routine” button on today’s Journal page. Chimes can now ring through Sleep Focus if you allow SpeckLog’s time-sensitive notifications.
  • Weekly goals, looked back on: at the end of the week SpeckLog asks whether you met your goal (green, yellow or red), and each goal shows its colored dot.
  • Backups now include your skincare products (with photos), routines and weekly goals.
  • Your week, in detail: tap the Your week card for a summary of the week so far: spots compared with last week, your best day, skincare and routines, your goal and check-in highlights.
  • Two reminders a day (Settings → Reminders), for a morning and an evening photo.
  • Your marks now fill in both spot counts in a new check-in (instead of yesterday’s numbers), two photos on one day fill the whole frame, and the Calendar’s month title looks like the button it is.
  • Ghost photo: choose your last photo or any photo you like (from any date) as the see-through guide in the camera. The crosshair now sits at eye level.
  • Minimalist mode moved under Descriptions and now keeps the good stuff (Patterns, recaps, goals, treatments) while hiding the extras. There may be a secret in it. 🙃
  • Thinner black borders on box marks, and “Hide descriptions” stays exactly where you tapped it.
  • Vibration is on by default for new installs.
  • Weekly goals: set a goal for the week on your Journal; once saved it’s locked in, and you can look back at every week’s goal.
  • Crosshair lines in the camera, to keep your face centered.
  • How SpeckLog Works: the method behind Patterns and experiments, in plain words (Settings → Help).
  • Photo notes in the camera: “Bathroom window, morning, light off”, shown every time you take a photo.
  • Reorder a day’s photos: ••• → Move Earlier in Day / Move Later in Day.
  • The check-in now asks about sleep before food & drink.
  • Your week, every day: under the check-in, see how many days you’ve logged this week and your average spots over the last 7 days. Sundays still open with the full weekly recap.
  • Delete Days now sends the days to Recently Deleted for 30 days, photos and check-ins, so a mistake can be undone.
  • New app icon: a warm salmon background with a crimson spot, so it looks like skin. The grey icon is still there in Settings → Appearance → App icon, next to the Light, Medium and Dark skin tones.

Line up your photos, spot map and Recently Deleted Latest

November 9, 2026, late night
  • Line up in Compare Days: move, resize and turn a photo with sliders (each with its own reset) until your face matches the reference. Save Position to Journal keeps it lined up in your Journal and time-lapse, so the video stops jumping. The photo itself isn’t changed; “Revert position” in its ••• menu puts it back.
  • Compare Days opens in Overlay, labels your days “Reference” and “To be positioned”, and can use any photo of a day (#1, #2…).
  • Last photo ghost: see your previous photo see-through in the camera (25–75%) to line your face up exactly. Switch between Off, Oval and Last photo right in the camera, with photo tips while you aim. Tap “Ghost: Nov 8” to match any earlier photo, not just the last one.
  • Events: log midterms, finals, travel or a busy week on the Calendar. They show on each day, and Patterns checks whether your skin changes around them.
  • Spot map in the Doctor Visit Report: every spot you marked in the period, shown as a heat map on your photo, so your doctor sees where breakouts keep coming back.
  • Recently Deleted: deleted photos wait 30 days in Settings → Data → Recently Deleted, with their marks and position, so you can bring them back.
  • Save to Photos from a photo’s ••• menu: as taken, with your marks, lined up, or both.
  • Tip jar: choose your own amount (any tip size, up to 10 at once), and see all your past tips with their dates. Already tipped? “Finish unlocking Plus” shows the tip that gets you the rest of the way, so tipping first never means paying for Plus on top.
  • Your marks can fill in the Small / mild count for you (Settings → Miscellaneous).
  • Delete just some days: one day, before or after a date, or a range (Settings → Data → Delete Days).
  • New Terms of Use, linked in Settings → About with the Privacy Policy.
  • Fixed: Settings could close the app when opened.
