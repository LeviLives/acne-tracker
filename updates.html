Line up your photos, spot map and Recently Deleted Latest

November 9, 2026, late night
  • Line up in Compare Days: move, resize and turn a photo with sliders (each with its own reset) until your face matches the reference. Save Position to Journal keeps it lined up in your Journal and time-lapse, so the video stops jumping. The photo itself isn’t changed; “Revert position” in its ••• menu puts it back.
  • Compare Days opens in Overlay, labels your days “Reference” and “To be positioned”, and can use any photo of a day (#1, #2…).
  • Last photo ghost: see your previous photo see-through in the camera (25–75%) to line your face up exactly. Switch between Off, Oval and Last photo right in the camera, with photo tips while you aim. Tap “Ghost: Nov 8” to match any earlier photo, not just the last one.
  • Events: log midterms, finals, travel or a busy week on the Calendar. They show on each day, and Patterns checks whether your skin changes around them.
  • Spot map in the Doctor Visit Report: every spot you marked in the period, shown as a heat map on your photo, so your doctor sees where breakouts keep coming back.
  • Recently Deleted: deleted or replaced photos wait 30 days in Settings → Data → Recently Deleted, with their marks and position, so you can bring them back.
  • Save to Photos from a photo’s ••• menu: as taken, with your marks, lined up, or both.
  • Your marks can fill in the Small / mild count for you (Settings → Miscellaneous).
  • Delete just some days: one day, before or after a date, or a range (Settings → Data → Delete Days).
  • New Terms of Use, linked in Settings → About with the Privacy Policy.
  • Fixed: Settings could close the app when opened.

Journal rewards and the weekly recap

November 9, 2026, night
  • Your first log of the day (a photo or a check-in) is celebrated: the streak badge pops, fire flies out, the phone buzzes and it says “Day 5 logged ✓”.
  • A “Next” countdown under the week shows the closest thing to unlock (time-lapse, Patterns, streak and logging milestones, badges) plus two ideas for today. It folds away once you’ve logged.
  • Every Sunday: a recap of your week, with spots compared with last week, your best day and a free before & after of this Sunday next to last Sunday. A perfect week gets confetti.
  • A “Your first week” before & after on your 7th day of photos.
  • New check-ins start with your last spot counts (Settings → Miscellaneous to turn it off).
  • Tap the month on the Calendar to jump to any month.
  • Smoother Calendar scrolling: sideways swipes have to be clearly sideways to change month.
  • Clearer outlined text over page backgrounds, and outlined page titles.

Patterns, experiments, time-lapse and backups

November 8, 2026, afternoon
  • Patterns: plain-language hints about what tends to come before your breakouts, with honest “Strong hint” / “Possible” labels.
  • Experiments: avoid something for a few weeks and see if it made a real difference.
  • Insights charts: compare a habit with your spots, and see stress and sleep as colors.
  • Your own check-in items, like “Shaved” or “Traveled”.
  • Time-lapse videos of your daily photos with a before & after.
  • Backups to a file (optionally password-protected) to move to a new iPhone.
  • Star and flower marks, and a calendar you can swipe through.
