What’s new in SpeckLog

What changed in each version of SpeckLog. SpeckLog is made by one person who has acne too, and uses it every day.

Version 1.1

In progress · Coming after 1.0

Import past photos

  • 📥Import past photos: bring in photos from Photos, a whole album, the Files app or a .zip. They go to the day they were taken. Nov 12
  • Unsorted: give undated photos a day, or add all photos to the day they were taken, any time. Nov 12
  • Mark a few a day: SpeckLog can ask you to mark a few imported photos each day. Nov 12
  • Importing the same photo twice? SpeckLog spots duplicates and offers to delete them. Nov 12
  • Undo an import: right after it finishes, or later from Recent Imports. Undone photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days, or delete them right away to free space. Stop and Undo cancels an import in progress. Nov 12
  • See how much space SpeckLog’s photos and files take: Settings → Data → Storage. Import Past Photos shows each album’s size and the total size of what you import. Nov 12

Early looks and research

  • 🔎Early looks: a first look at 7 days and early hints at 14 days, with a progress bar in Patterns and Insights. Early hints are marked “Too early to be sure”. Nov 12
  • Experiments can be a 1-week quick check (less certain). Nov 12
  • Today’s research: a real acne study each day, with a link. Absurd mode adds odd studies and jokes. Nov 12
  • 5 new badges for opening and exporting charts. Nov 12

A calmer start

  • 🌱A simpler start: new users see features as they log enough days for them, with a 🎉 unlock when each one appears. Existing users keep everything. Nov 12
  • Past days you didn’t log stay clean. Nov 12
  • Daily reminders are offered right after your first log. Nov 12
  • Feature suggestions: Weekly, Monthly or Never. Nov 12

Polish

  • Hygiene now appears on every day page, and each item can be asked daily or again after a number of days. Nov 12
  • The Routine card is tidier, the month title is cleaner and card titles have a divider. Nov 12
  • The pen outline is a little heavier and closer to the sticker color. The black border now works on the pen and stickers too. The placed shapes are now called stickers. Nov 12
  • Charts load faster. Insights is much lighter: turning a habit on or off in Totals redraws only that line. Long check-in item names no longer push the Compare chart out of place. Nov 12
  • Chinese: dates read 2026年11月7日 everywhere, more screens are fully in Chinese and the wording is consistent. Nov 12

Apple Watch and routines

  • ⌚SpeckLog on Apple Watch: run your skincare routines from your wrist, with a countdown and a gentle tap at the end of each step. Confirm at the end and it’s logged on your iPhone. A routine started on your iPhone shows on the watch too, and your streak sits at the top. Nov 11 · afternoon
  • Skincare routines with a timer: morning and evening routines with steps (wash, wait 10 minutes, treatment, moisturizer, take your medicine…). Start one and it runs itself, with a chime at each step, even with the screen off. Confirm at the end and it’s logged. Nov 10 · late night
  • Routines, upgraded: Morning, Evening or General routines for Home or Travel; a short “Next: …” countdown between steps; several products per step (all logged when you finish); a Previous button; a chime when you start; and a “Start morning / evening routine” button on today’s Journal page. Chimes can now ring through Sleep Focus if you allow SpeckLog’s time-sensitive notifications. Nov 11 · late night
  • Routines with a choice: add a step with two paths (like “Shower first” or “Just moisturizer”) and the routine asks which way when it gets there. Nov 11 · afternoon
  • Background sound for routines: soft, calm tones while your routine runs, under the chimes. Turn it on per routine or right in the run screen. Nov 11 · afternoon

Names and brands capitalize themselves, the product camera uses the back camera, and nothing you type is lost if you scroll or drag the sheet. Nov 11 · late night
