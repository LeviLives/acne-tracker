Getting started

How do I use SpeckLog each day?

Open the Journal tab, take a photo (or pick one from your library), mark your spots, then do the check-in: spot counts, stress, sleep, food and drink. It takes about a minute. The welcome tour (Settings → Help → Take the Tour) shows where everything is.

Photos & marks

How do I mark spots?

After adding a photo, the marking screen opens. With the Star (or Box / Flower) tool, tap to drop a mark or drag to size one. With the Pen, paint over an area. The Eraser removes marks, and Pan moves the photo. Pinch to zoom with any tool. To adjust a mark, tap it: drag the round handle above it to turn it, the corner to resize it, and the handle below it to move it. Tap the photo on the day page later to edit its marks.

Can I change how marks look?

Yes: Settings → Appearance → More options → Marks on photos, or the style button in the marking screen. Star and red Box are free; Flower and other box colors come with SpeckLog Plus. Only the drawing changes: every mark keeps its place and size.

I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days: Settings → Data → Recently Deleted. Tap the photo and choose Restore to Its Day; it comes back with its marks and position. Replaced photos go there too. After 30 days they're erased for good.

Check-in

How should I count spots?

Large / painful = deep, sore or swollen spots; Small / mild = everything else. Tap the arrows or type the number. Your best guess is fine: counting the same way each day matters more than being exact.

Can I track my own things?

Yes. Add yes/no items such as “Shaved” or “Traveled” at the bottom of the check-in or in Settings → Check-in items. They show up in Insights, Patterns and experiments, counted from the day you added them. Removing an item only hides it, so its history stays.

Patterns & Insights

How do Patterns work?

SpeckLog turns each habit into how often you did it over the last 7 days, then compares that with your spot counts 0 to 7 days later. Two checks are used:

One at a time: a correlation for each habit at each delay. Because habits and skin come in stretches, neighbouring days aren't independent, so SpeckLog works out an effective number of days (often 15–30 out of 180) and uses a cautious range so chance alone rarely shows up as a link.

All together: a regression with every habit at once, so habits that change together (stressful weeks with more fast food) share the credit instead of each looking linked.

Patterns need about 6 weeks of check-ins with spot counts. All of it runs on your iPhone. The formulas are in the app under Settings → Patterns & Insights → “The math behind it”.

What do “Strong hint” and “Possible” mean?

Strong hint: both checks find the link and agree on its direction, or the all-together model is very sure on its own. Possible: one check finds it and the other doesn't clearly disagree. If the two checks point opposite ways, the link is dropped: it's probably another habit that moves with it.

Neither proves a cause. The best test is an experiment: tap Test it under a hint.

How do I read the Insights charts?

Compare picks one habit: each day is a blue column, darker when you had it on more days of that week, with your spot counts drawn on top. “Shift habit days later” moves the columns if your skin reacts a few days after. Under the chart, a sentence compares spots on weeks with the habit and weeks without.

Totals counts each habit up through the period (a steeper line = more often), with spots as dots on the right axis and stress & sleep shading behind. Daily status shows stress and sleep as a color strip.

Experiments & treatments

How do experiments work?

Pick a habit to avoid (or have daily) for 2, 3, 4 or 6 weeks. SpeckLog compares your spots during the experiment with the same number of days just before it, skipping the first week of a longer one because skin takes days to respond. It accounts for neighbouring days being alike, and says either “a real change” or “could be chance”. It also shows how many logged days you kept to it.

How do I log a treatment like Accutane?

Use the Treatments card on the Calendar. Each day's page then shows the week of the course, and the time-lapse can show it too. You can add side effects (dry lips, dry skin…) as check-in items. SpeckLog only records; always follow your doctor's instructions.

Reports, compare & widgets

How do I make a report for my doctor?

Calendar → Doctor Report (SpeckLog Plus) makes a PDF: spot counts at the start and end of the period with a weekly chart, your treatments, finished experiments, Patterns' hints, how often you logged each food, sleep and stress answer, and a few key photos with your marks. Pick the period (4 weeks to all time) and how many photos, then share, print or AirDrop it. It's made on your iPhone and goes nowhere unless you share it.

How do I compare two days?

Calendar → Compare Days (SpeckLog Plus). Pick a before and an after day. Side by side shows both photos; Overlay lays the later one over the earlier one at 50%, so spots that came or went look faint. The Blend slider moves between them. Photos line up best when taken the same way each day: use the Oval or Last photo guide at the top of the camera.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen (or the Lock Screen, then Customize), tap + and search for SpeckLog. There's a Streak widget (your streak and whether you've checked in today) and a Spot trend widget (the last 14 days). Widgets come with SpeckLog Plus.

Can I lock SpeckLog with Face ID?

Yes, on iOS 18 and later: touch and hold the SpeckLog icon and choose Require Face ID. iOS then asks for Face ID (or your passcode) every time SpeckLog opens and hides it from the app switcher preview.

Time-lapse

How do I make a time-lapse?

Tap Time-lapse at the top right of the Calendar. It uses the first photo of each day, so you need photos on 14 days (from 2 days you can make a short one anyway). Choose the dates, marks, dates on screen, a before & after ending and speed, then tap Make Video and save or share it.

Backups & data

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

An iCloud backup of your iPhone, or a direct iPhone-to-iPhone transfer, brings SpeckLog's data along. Otherwise, go to Settings → Data → Export Backup, which makes one file with all your photos and check-ins (optionally password-protected). Save it to Files or AirDrop it, then open it on the other iPhone and choose Merge or Replace.

If you set a password and forget it, the file can't be opened. Nobody, including us, can recover it.

How does iCloud Sync work?

It’s free. Turn on Settings → Data → iCloud Sync on each iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Your photos, marks, check-ins, experiments, treatments and your own items are kept in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read; we never see it. The first sync of many photos can take a while and needs iCloud storage space. Turn it off any time; to remove the iCloud copy, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpeckLog. Settings like colors stay per iPhone.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data → Delete All Data. It offers to export a backup first, since deleting can't be undone. Deleting the app also removes its data.

SpeckLog Plus & tips

What does SpeckLog Plus add?

Extras on top of the free app: a doctor visit report (PDF), Compare Days (side by side or overlaid), Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, more theme, outline and mark colors, page backgrounds, title emoji and a time-lapse without the “Made with SpeckLog” credit. It's a one-time purchase, and new Plus features come with it. Tracking, Patterns, Insights, experiments, backups, the face outline guide and all app icons stay free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the SpeckLog Plus screen or the Tip Jar in Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Tips that add up to the Plus price unlock Plus too, and Restore finds them on iOS 18 and later.

Reminders & settings

Why did my reminders stop?

Reminders are off until you turn them on in Settings. Once on, SpeckLog schedules at most the next 3 days and skips days you've already logged, so they pause after 3 days without opening the app and restart when you open it. Also check iPhone Settings → Notifications → SpeckLog and any Focus mode.

Privacy

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos and check-ins are stored only on your iPhone, there's no account, and Patterns and Insights are worked out on the device. We don't collect analytics, usage data or crash reports. Purchases go through Apple, and we never see your payment details. Read the full privacy policy.

Is SpeckLog medical advice?

No, it's a diary. For painful, cystic or scarring acne, see a doctor or dermatologist.
