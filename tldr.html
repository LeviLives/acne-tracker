TL;DR · SpeckLog 1.1 for iPhone

SpeckLog, at a glance

Track acne, see progress and keep your care together.

Make today easy

Add a daily photo, mark spots and tap through a check-in. Capture sleep, stress and habits without writing a diary.

See what changed

Follow one spot up close or watch your photos become a time-lapse. Look back with pictures, not just memory.

Start small, grow naturally

A fresh journal starts simple. Useful tools appear as your history builds. Optional reminders help you return to it.

Keep care together

Keep skincare routines, treatments and visit documents in one place. Run a routine on Apple Watch; bring a Plus report to your clinician.

Your journal stays yours

Stored on your iPhone by default. Optional private iCloud sync. No SpeckLog account, ads or tracking; the developer cannot read your journal.

Free core. One-time extras.

Photos, check-ins, Insights and Patterns stay free. Optional Plus adds Compare Days, widgets, doctor reports and more looks. No subscription.

SpeckLog supports personal recordkeeping. For medical advice, talk to a clinician.
